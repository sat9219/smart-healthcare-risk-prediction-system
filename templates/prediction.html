🧠 AI Clinical Diagnostic Architecture

The prediction engine calculates individualized probabilities using Gradient Boosting & Random Forest models calibrated against standard clinical guidelines:

  • 🩸

    Type 2 Diabetes Risk Index

    Calculated via American Diabetes Association (ADA) criteria correlating fasting glycemia, age, BMI, and physical activity.

    Cardiovascular Morbidity

    Framingham risk matrix evaluating lipid profile, resting cardiac rhythm, and arterial pulse pressure.

  • 🩺

    Hypertension Staging

    ACC/AHA 2017 staging guidelines evaluating systolic/diastolic thresholds, vascular elasticity, and lifestyle factors.
