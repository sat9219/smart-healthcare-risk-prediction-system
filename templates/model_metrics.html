🎓 Final Year Project Defense & Architecture Justification

Why Random Forest Classifier as Primary Model?
Medical diagnostic indicators exhibit non-linear interactions (e.g., elevated BMI combined with positive family history accelerates glycemic risk disproportionately). Random Forest uses an ensemble of 120 decorrelated decision trees with bootstrap aggregation (bagging), preventing overfitting and providing superior generalization over linear models while preserving interpretable Gini feature attribution for Explainable AI (XAI).

  • Stratified 5-Fold Validation: Preserves equal positive-negative ratio across risk strata.
  • Class Balance Handling: Probability calibration guarantees smooth clinical threshold mapping (Low <30%, Medium 30-59%, High ≥60%).
  • Real-Time Inference: Serialized `.joblib` pipelines execute prediction in <15ms per patient request.
